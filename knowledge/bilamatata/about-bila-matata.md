# About Bila Matata

Bila Matata ("without the hassle") is an AI, software development, data analysis and research studio based in Mombasa, Kenya. We help businesses capture more leads, serve customers faster and make better decisions, without adding to their workload.

## What we do

- **Zaina AI**: a 24/7 AI sales and booking concierge for a business's website and WhatsApp.
- **Website and mobile app development**: business websites, online shops and apps for Android and iPhone.
- **AI automation and workflow systems**: the repeated work a team does every day, done automatically.
- **Data analysis and business intelligence**: dashboards and analysis from the data a business already has.
- **Strategic research and professional writing**: market and feasibility research, business plans, proposals and reports.

## Who we work with

Small and medium businesses, organisations and founders across Kenya, and clients abroad who work with us remotely. Typical clients are service businesses that get many enquiries: hotels, lodges and villas, salons, clinics, restaurants, shops, schools, property managers, consultants and online sellers.

# How we work, payments and refunds

## Starting a project

1. Tell us what you need, on WhatsApp or in this chat.
2. We send a written quote: scope, price and delivery date. A quote is valid for 30 days.
3. You pay half of the quoted amount to start the work.
4. We deliver, you review, we make the agreed revisions, and the other half is due on delivery.

Work outside the quote is quoted separately before we start it. After delivery you have 7 days to tell us about anything that doesn't match the quote, and we fix it at no charge.

## Zaina AI billing

- A one-off set-up fee is paid before set-up begins.
- The monthly fee is billed in advance.
- You can cancel at any time. The plan runs to the end of the month already paid for; there is no cancellation fee.
- If an invoice stays unpaid after its grace period, Zaina is paused until it is paid. Data is kept during the pause.

## How to pay

- Project invoices and Zaina set-up fees: at https://bilamatata.co.ke/pay with your invoice reference (it starts with BM-), by M-Pesa or card.
- Zaina monthly invoices: from your dashboard at https://bilamatata.co.ke/app/ under Settings, then Plan & billing.
- Payments are processed by Paystack. Bila Matata never sees your card number or M-Pesa PIN.
- A receipt is emailed after each payment.

## Refunds

- Paid twice or charged the wrong amount: refunded in full.
- Project deposit: refunded in full if you cancel before work starts. Once work has started, the deposit covers the work done so far.
- If we cannot deliver what was quoted, we refund the part not delivered.
- Zaina set-up fee: refundable before set-up begins; not refundable once Zaina has been set up and shown to you.
- Zaina monthly fee: not refunded for part of a month.

To ask for a refund, email hello@bilamatata.co.ke with your invoice reference.

## Ownership

Once a project is paid in full, the work created for the client belongs to the client.

## Privacy

We handle personal data under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Each Zaina client's conversations are kept separate, and conversations are deleted after the retention period (90 days unless agreed otherwise). The full terms and privacy notice are at https://bilamatata.co.ke/terms

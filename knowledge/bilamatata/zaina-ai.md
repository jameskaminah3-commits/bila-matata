# Zaina AI: a 24/7 AI sales and booking concierge

Zaina is an AI assistant that Bila Matata sets up for a business. She answers the business's customers on its website and WhatsApp at any hour, helps them find what they need, and turns conversations into leads and bookings.

You are talking to a Zaina right now: this one is set up for Bila Matata itself.

## How Zaina works

- **She answers only from the business's own information.** Everything Zaina says comes directly from the business: its catalogue, its price list, its policies and its up-to-date calendar. She does not answer from the internet or from general knowledge about other businesses.
- **She does not invent prices.** Every price Zaina quotes comes straight from the business's own price list. If a price is not in the list, she says so and asks the team.
- **She does not guess.** If the answer is not in the business's information, Zaina says so, offers to take the customer's details and alerts the team.
- **She checks the up-to-date calendar.** Before offering a time or a date, Zaina checks what is actually free.
- **She helps customers find and book.** Zaina helps each customer find the option that suits them, then makes the booking and takes the deposit.
- **A person can always step in.** The team sees every conversation and can take over any chat.

## What a business gets from Zaina

- **No more missed enquiries.** Zaina replies in seconds, day and night, including weekends and late at night.
- **Answers the business can stand behind.** Nothing is made up: answers and prices come from the business itself.
- **Leads that are ready to close.** Zaina collects the customer's name, phone number and what they want, and alerts the team.
- **Bookings without the back-and-forth.** On the Full plan, Zaina checks the up-to-date calendar, quotes the exact price, creates the booking and takes the deposit by M-Pesa or card.
- **A team free for real work.** Repeat questions are answered automatically. When a customer needs a person, Zaina hands the whole conversation to the team.

## How Zaina differs from a generic chatbot

- A generic chatbot answers from scripted menus or general knowledge. Zaina answers directly from the business's own information.
- A generic chatbot can answer confidently with something untrue. Zaina says when she doesn't have the answer and brings in the team.
- A generic chatbot guesses prices or says "contact us". Zaina quotes only the prices in the business's price list.
- A generic chatbot cannot see the calendar. Zaina checks the business's up-to-date calendar before offering a time.
- A generic chatbot sends a form or a link. Zaina helps the customer choose, creates the booking and takes the deposit, paid into the business's own payment account.
- Zaina hands over to a person with the full conversation, and a team member can take over any chat.
- Zaina speaks English and Swahili.
- Zaina works on the business's website and on its WhatsApp number, with one shared inbox.
- Bila Matata sets Zaina up: the business does not have to build chat flows.

## The two plans

- **Starter Sales Concierge**: answers customers 24/7 from the catalogue and price list, captures leads and hands them to the team on WhatsApp or in the dashboard.
- **Full Booking & Quote Engine**: everything in Starter, plus up-to-date calendar checks, exact quotes including bundles, bookings created from start to finish, and deposits by M-Pesa or card.

Each plan has a monthly price and a one-off set-up fee. Use the price list for the current amounts.

## What set-up includes

We load the business's catalogue, prices, opening hours and policies, tune Zaina's answers and tone with the owner, connect the business's own payment account and calendars where the plan includes them, and install the chat on the website. The website installation is one line of code and works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix and custom-built sites.

## The dashboard

Clients sign in at https://bilamatata.co.ke/app/ to read conversations, reply to customers, see leads and bookings, update their catalogue, prices and calendar, and view reports. When the business updates its prices or calendar, Zaina uses the new information straight away.

## Customer payments

When Zaina takes a deposit from a business's customer, the money goes straight into that business's own payment account (M-Pesa or card). Bila Matata does not hold a client's customer payments.

## Results

Zaina makes sure every enquiry is answered and followed up. How many leads and sales a business gets also depends on its offer and its market, so we do not promise a particular number.

# Zaina AI: a 24/7 AI sales and booking concierge

Zaina is an AI assistant that Bila Matata sets up for a business. It answers the business's customers on its website and WhatsApp at any hour, using the business's own catalogue, price list and policies, and turns conversations into leads and bookings.

You are talking to a Zaina right now: this one is set up for Bila Matata itself.

## What a business gets from Zaina

- **No more missed enquiries.** Zaina replies in seconds, day and night, including weekends and late at night.
- **Leads that are ready to close.** Zaina collects the customer's name, phone number and what they want, and alerts the team.
- **Bookings without the back-and-forth.** On the Full plan, Zaina checks live availability, quotes the exact price, creates the booking and takes the deposit by M-Pesa or card.
- **A team free for real work.** Repeat questions are answered automatically. When a customer needs a person, Zaina hands the whole conversation to the team.

## How Zaina differs from a generic chatbot

- It answers from the business's own information, not from scripted menus or general knowledge.
- Prices come from the business's price list. If a price isn't in the list, Zaina says so and asks the team instead of guessing.
- It can check the business's live calendar before offering a time.
- It can create a booking and take a deposit, paid into the business's own account.
- It hands over to a person with the full conversation, and a team member can take over any chat.
- It speaks English and Swahili.
- It works on the business's website and on its WhatsApp number, with one shared inbox.
- Bila Matata sets it up: the business does not have to build chat flows.

## The two plans

- **Starter Sales Concierge**: answers customers 24/7 from the catalogue and price list, captures leads and hands them to the team on WhatsApp or in the dashboard.
- **Full Booking & Quote Engine**: everything in Starter, plus live availability checks, exact quotes including bundles, bookings created from start to finish, and deposits by M-Pesa or card.

Each plan has a monthly price and a one-off set-up fee. Use the price list for the current amounts.

## What set-up includes

We load the business's catalogue, prices, opening hours and policies, tune Zaina's answers and tone with the owner, connect payments and calendars where the plan includes them, and install the chat on the website. The website installation is one line of code and works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix and custom-built sites.

## The dashboard

Clients sign in at https://bilamatata.co.ke/app/ to read conversations, reply to customers, see leads and bookings, update their catalogue and prices, and view reports.

## Customer payments

When Zaina takes a deposit from a business's customer, the money goes straight into that business's own Paystack or M-Pesa account. Bila Matata does not hold a client's customer payments.

## Limits to be honest about

Zaina is an AI system and can make mistakes. It is built to answer from the business's own information and to hand over to a person when unsure. We do not promise a particular number of leads or sales.

# Zaina AI: a 24/7 AI sales and booking concierge

Zaina is the digital team member that Bila Matata sets up for a business. From the day she starts she works as one of its people, with a role on its team (a guest experience host at a place to stay, a client care host at a salon, a reservations host at a restaurant, and so on). She answers the business's customers on its website and WhatsApp at any hour, helps them find what they need, and turns conversations into leads and bookings.

You are talking to a Zaina right now: this one is set up for Bila Matata itself.

## Who Zaina is on a business's team

- **She is a team member, not a generic bot.** She speaks for the business as one of its people: "our rooms", "we open at 8". She introduces herself by name and role, for example "Hi, I'm Zaina, the guest experience host at Palm Cove Stays."
- **She has a face and a name.** Customers see her picture on the chat button and in the chat, so the business has a friendly, recognisable host on its website.
- **The business chooses her role.** In Settings the business can name her role (for example "front desk host") and see how it looks to customers.
- **She is honest about what she is.** She never pretends to be a human. If a customer sincerely asks whether they are talking to a person or a bot, she says warmly that she is the team's digital team member (an AI) and that the people on the team are right behind her if they would like one.

## How Zaina works

- **She understands what the customer needs.** Zaina works out what each customer is really after, asks a short question when one detail is missing, and recommends the option that suits them from what the business actually sells. When the business offers an extra or upgrade that genuinely fits, she suggests it at its listed price. She always offers a next step: a time to book, a quote, or a call from the team.
- **She answers only from the business's own information.** Everything Zaina says comes directly from the business: its catalogue, its price list, its policies and its up-to-date calendar. She does not answer from the internet or from general knowledge about other businesses.
- **She does not invent prices.** Every price Zaina quotes comes straight from the business's own price list. If a price is not in the list, she says so and asks the team.
- **She does not guess.** If the answer is not in the business's information, Zaina says so, offers to take the customer's details and alerts the team.
- **She checks the up-to-date calendar.** Before offering a time or a date, Zaina checks what is actually free.
- **She helps customers find and book.** Zaina helps each customer find the option that suits them, then makes the booking and sends the business's own payment link for the deposit.
- **A person can always step in.** The team sees every conversation and can take over any chat.

## What a business gets from Zaina

- **No more missed enquiries.** Zaina replies in seconds, day and night, including weekends and late at night.
- **Answers the business can stand behind.** Nothing is made up: answers and prices come from the business itself.
- **Leads that are ready to close.** Zaina collects the customer's name, phone number and what they want, and alerts the team.
- **Bookings without the back-and-forth.** On the Full plan, Zaina checks the up-to-date calendar, quotes the exact price, creates the booking and sends the business's own M-Pesa or card payment link.
- **A team free for real work.** Repeat questions are answered automatically. When a customer needs a person, Zaina hands the whole conversation to the team.

## How Zaina compares with auto-responders and basic chatbots

Auto-responders and basic chatbots leave customers waiting for someone to check availability and work out the price. Zaina is wired into the business's own rates, calendar and checkout, so she answers, quotes and books on the spot.

- **Availability.** An auto-responder says "we'll check and get back to you", and impatient buyers go elsewhere. Zaina checks the business's up-to-date calendar the moment a customer asks, and offers only the times or dates that are really free.
- **Price quotes.** A basic bot sends a static price list or guesses, so staff still work out the total. Zaina works out the exact total from the business's own rates, for the dates, the number of people and the business's fees. She never guesses a figure.
- **Answers you can trust.** A basic bot guesses or answers from general knowledge. Zaina answers only from the business's own information, and says so when something isn't there.
- **Recommendations and extras.** A basic bot only answers what was asked. Zaina recommends what suits each customer and suggests the extras and upgrades the business really offers, at the listed prices, without being pushy.
- **Booking and checkout.** A basic bot stops at chatting. Zaina creates the booking and sends the M-Pesa or card checkout, 24/7, paid into the business's own account.
- **Hand-off.** A basic bot traps frustrated customers in a loop. Zaina passes complex or VIP requests to the team with the whole conversation and the customer's details, and alerts them on their phones.

Availability, exact booking totals, booking and checkout come with the Full Booking & Quote Engine plan.

Zaina also speaks English and Swahili, and Bila Matata sets her up: the business does not have to build anything.

## A QR code and page for every business

A business does not need a website to use Zaina. Every business gets its own page with its name, logo and colours, where customers chat with Zaina, and a QR code that opens it. From the dashboard (Settings, then QR code & link) the business prints the code as a poster, table cards or stickers, downloads it, or shares the link on WhatsApp and social media. The dashboard shows how many people scanned the code and how many started a chat.

### Where a scan goes, and business cards

The printed code never changes, and the business chooses where a scan goes: its own page, its website with the chat already open (once the chat is set up there), or its WhatsApp number (once it is connected to Zaina). The code can carry the business's colour and logo in the centre, and stays fully readable, or be plain black and white.

The business can also make premium business cards in the dashboard (Settings, then Business cards): its brand on the front, its QR code on the back, in three designs. They print ten to an A4 sheet at the standard 85 by 55 mm, or the business can ask Bila Matata to print and deliver them, with a price agreed before anything is printed. Card scans are counted separately.

## The business's own brand

In the dashboard (Settings, then Brand) a business adds its logo, a cover picture, its colour and a welcome line, and chooses whether the chat shows Zaina's face or its logo. The brand appears on the chat, on the business's page behind its QR code, and on the page where its customers pay.

## Notification emails

A business decides who is told about its chats. In the dashboard (Settings, then Notifications) the owner or a manager adds the email addresses that should hear when a customer is waiting for a person, wants a callback, leaves their details or books. For each address the business chooses what it gets: an alert with a link to open the chat and take over, the conversation written out, or both. Addresses can be changed, tested and removed at any time.

How many addresses a business can add depends on its plan: Starter allows up to 2, and Full allows up to 5.

## Hotels, lodges, villas and stays

Hotels, lodges, villas and other places to stay are a key kind of business for Zaina. On the Full plan she answers guests' questions from the property's own information, checks the property's up-to-date calendar (including calendars imported from other booking channels), quotes the exact total from the property's own rates (nightly rates, seasonal rates, extra-guest charges and fees), holds the room, sends the property's own M-Pesa or card payment link so the deposit is paid straight to the property, and passes the booking to the team. When the dates a guest wants are taken, she offers the nearest option the property really has instead of losing the guest. A late-night enquiry can be a confirmed, paid booking by morning.

## The two plans

- **Starter Sales Concierge**: answers customers 24/7 from the catalogue and price list, captures leads and hands them to the team on WhatsApp or in the dashboard. Up to 2 notification emails.
- **Full Booking & Quote Engine**: everything in Starter, plus up-to-date calendar checks, exact totals from the business's own rates (dates, number of people and fees), bookings created from start to finish, and the M-Pesa or card checkout. Up to 5 notification emails.

Each plan has a monthly price and a one-off set-up fee. Use the price list for the current amounts.

## The dashboard

Clients sign in at https://bilamatata.co.ke/app/ to read conversations, reply to customers, see leads and bookings, update their catalogue, prices and calendar, and view reports. When the business updates its prices or calendar, Zaina uses the new information straight away.

## Customer payments

Zaina never collects or holds money for a business. When a deposit is due, she sends the customer the business's own payment link, and the money goes straight into that business's own payment account (M-Pesa or card). Bila Matata does not hold a client's customer payments.

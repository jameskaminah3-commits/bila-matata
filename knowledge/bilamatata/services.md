# Studio services

Besides Zaina AI, Bila Matata takes on custom work in four areas. Every project starts with what it should change for the client's business, followed by a written quote with a fixed scope, price and delivery date.

## Website and mobile app development

A site or app that brings in enquiries and takes payments.

- Business websites and online shops
- Customer and staff apps for Android and iPhone
- M-Pesa and card payments built in
- Fast, mobile-first pages that search engines can read

## AI automation and workflow systems

The copying, chasing and re-typing a team does every day, done for them.

- Enquiries routed and answered automatically
- Invoices, reminders and follow-ups that send themselves
- Tools connected to each other: forms, spreadsheets, WhatsApp, email
- Custom AI assistants for internal use

## Data analysis and business intelligence

Know what sells, what it costs and where money leaks, from data the business already has.

- Dashboards a whole team can read
- Sales, stock and customer analysis
- Clean-up of messy spreadsheets and records
- Surveys designed and analysed

## Strategic research and professional writing

Decisions and documents backed by evidence.

- Market and feasibility research
- Business plans, proposals and grant applications
- Reports, company profiles and policy documents

We report what the evidence shows. We cannot promise that a proposal or application will be approved or funded.
